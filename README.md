# Sofia's LinkedIn Writing Skill

A reusable skill for AI assistants that draft LinkedIn posts in Sofia's personal voice.

## What it does
- Writes concise, warm, natural English LinkedIn posts.
- Covers ultrasound repair cases, technical guides, company news, spare parts, industry observations, and trade shows.
- Keeps Sofia positioned as an overseas sales professional who works with engineers—not as the engineer performing repairs.
- Prioritizes real customer context, useful information, low-pressure CTAs, and factual accuracy.
- Avoids generic AI copy, corporate press-release language, and hard selling.

## Files
- `SKILL.md` — main instructions.
- `examples.md` — examples of the preferred voice.

## Use
For AI systems that support skills or instruction files, import/reference `SKILL.md`. Otherwise, paste its contents into the system prompt or custom instructions.

## Example request
> Write a concise LinkedIn post based on this repair case. Keep it natural and warm, use short paragraphs, don't make me sound like an engineer, and only use the facts provided.

Add new examples when Sofia approves a post that closely matches her preferred voice. Do not add customer-sensitive details without permission.
